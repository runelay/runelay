Google Slides on Runelay, an open source MCP gateway

Connect Google Slides once. Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and Grok all use that connection, and the credential never leaves your server.

What this connection does

Create, read and edit presentations. Runelay connects to the Google Slides remote MCP server at https://slidesmcp.googleapis.com/mcp/v1 and puts its tools behind your own endpoint.

Agents find them with search_tools and run them with call_tool, so the Google Slides tool list is not loaded into context until it is needed.

Use Google Slides in your agents

Agents do not need a Google Slides entry of their own. Point each one at Runelay and Google Slides is there.

Questions

Does my Google Slides credential reach the agent?

No. It stays on your Runelay server, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your RUNELAY_SECRET. Agents only hold their Runelay key.

Do I connect Google Slides once per agent?

No. You connect it once in Runelay, and every agent that points at your endpoint uses the same connection.

I already have Google Slides set up in an agent. Can I bring it?

Yes. runelay migrate reads the MCP servers your agents already have. If the CLI can read the token it arrives connected; otherwise it waits in the reconnect queue in the panel.
