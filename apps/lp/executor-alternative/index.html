Runelay fits when

  • Your own agents should share accounts, skills and memory.
  • Everything must run on infrastructure you control.
  • You already have MCP servers and memories in your agents and want to import them.
  • Context and RAM are tight.

Executor fits when

  • You want a hosted cloud you do not operate.
  • A team needs members, verified domains and billing per member.
  • You need SSO, SCIM or a dedicated deployment from a vendor.

Questions

Is Runelay a fork of Executor?

No. Runelay is a separate MIT project written with Bun, Hono, SQLite and React.

Can I move from Executor to Runelay?

Runelay accepts any remote MCP URL, so you can add the servers you use one by one. runelay migrate imports what is configured in your agents, not what is stored inside Executor.

Are the benchmark numbers current?

They are from 14 Nov 2026 with Runelay 0.3.2 and Executor 2.0.0-beta.51. Both projects change quickly, so check the benchmark page for the versions tested.
